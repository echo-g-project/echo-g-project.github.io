ECHO-G generates full-body humanoid co-speech motion from speech audio and timed transcripts. Its Speech-Grounded Diffusion Transformer (SGDiT) combines frame-aligned acoustic features with token-level linguistic context, retaining their distinct granularities.

Rectified flow matching models multiple plausible motions for the same utterance. The generated joint-position references are executed by a fixed whole-body motion tracker.

The project also introduces a BEAT2-derived audio–text–robot dataset and a benchmark covering co-speech characteristics, robot-motion quality, and runtime efficiency.
